# Medical-Devices-Sales-Analysis

### 🎯 Objective
Evaluate regional sales performance and device-level profitability across North America, Europe, APAC, and LATAM to uncover cost optimization opportunities and improve overall profit margins.

---

### 🧹 Data Preparation
- Consolidated multiple regional sales extracts into a unified dataset.
- Standardized currency and date formats for cross-market consistency.
- Aggregated device-level metrics to calculate key KPIs: `Total_Revenue`, `Total_Units_Sold`, and `Avg_Of_Revenue`.
- Conducted validation checks for duplicates, missing entries, and outliers.

> ⚠️ *The original dataset is confidential. An anonymized sample structure is provided for demonstration purposes.*

### 💡 Business Insights
- Total revenue reached **$176.6M** across **50K units sold**.  
- Strongest market regions: **North America (38%)** and **Europe (29%)**.  
- **APAC** and **LATAM** remain underutilized, indicating efficiency gaps and potential for expansion.  
- Top-performing, high-margin devices: **StentX** and **OrthoKnee**.

---

### 💼 Recommendations
- Streamline vendor contracts for high-margin devices (*StentX*, *OrthoKnee*).  
- Reallocate operational resources toward emerging, high-growth regions.  
- Apply predictive modeling to align procurement cycles with regional demand trends.

---

### 📈 Projected Outcomes
- Reduce operating costs by **10–15 %**.  
- Increase profit margins by **≈ 12 %**.  
- Enhance supply-chain responsiveness and efficiency through data-driven optimization.
